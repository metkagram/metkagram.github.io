# Speaking expansion — wave 3

Added 40 more lexical-grammatical patterns in four additive study sets, each with five distinct scenarios aligned in English, German and Russian. This wave adds 200 scenarios / 600 sentence versions.

Across all three speaking-expansion waves, the branch now adds 200 patterns in 20 study sets: 1,000 aligned practice scenarios / 3,000 sentence versions. The catalogue grows from 930 to 1,130 patterns and from 109 to 129 study sets.

The four sets are:

- **SRQ — Polite requests and negotiation:** permission, soft requests, alternatives and low-pressure negotiation.
- **SDC — Contrast and qualification in discourse:** concession, emphasis shifts, distinctions and careful qualification.
- **SMN — Thinking and attention frames:** assumptions, blind spots, sense-making, attention and learning from evidence.
- **SRA — Responsibility, adaptation and recovery:** accountability, coping, adjustment, recovery and sustained commitment.

All 80 new EN/DE example lists are required to pass the repository's stricter C1 diversity floor. New formulas are checked structurally against the previous 1,090-pattern catalogue so the wave does not add formula-level duplicates. Russian learner translations stay aligned across English and German examples.

The examples rotate across household problems, repairs, speaking practice, music, travel, deliveries, community projects, archives, gardens, customer service and technical work. They are designed as reusable speaking practice rather than noun-substitution variants.

No dependency Marks are fabricated. Existing raw annotation records remain untouched.
